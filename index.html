How is this private?

Your password never leaves your device in plaintext. It's hashed locally with SHA-1 in your browser, then only the first 5 characters of that hash are sent to the HaveIBeenPwned Pwned Passwords API. HIBP returns every breached hash suffix that starts with those 5 characters — often thousands of them — and the match happens locally on your device. This is the same k-anonymity model used by Chrome, Firefox, and 1Password's breach checks.
